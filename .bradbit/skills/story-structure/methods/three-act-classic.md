# Classic three-act structure

## What it is
The most basic model (Aristotle via Syd Field): setup, confrontation,
resolution. It serves as a skeleton for almost any more detailed method.

## Acts
1. **Setup (~25%)**: world, character, inciting incident; end of act 1 =
   the character makes a decision they cannot undo.
2. **Confrontation (~50%)**: rising obstacles, a midpoint that raises the
   stakes, everything complicates until the lowest point.
3. **Resolution (~25%)**: climax, facing the central conflict, resolution
   and a new status quo.

## When it works best
When the user wants to work fast, without the granularity of a beat
sheet, or as a top layer before applying a more detailed method (for
example, place the 3 acts first and then fill each one with 8
sequences).
