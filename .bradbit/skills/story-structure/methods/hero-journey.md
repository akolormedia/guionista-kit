# Hero's Journey (Campbell / Vogler)

## What it is
A mythical structure of 12 (Vogler) or 17 (Campbell) stages, about a
protagonist who leaves their known world, is transformed through a trial,
and returns changed.

## Stages (Vogler version, 12 steps)
1. Ordinary world
2. Call to adventure
3. Refusal of the call
4. Meeting the mentor
5. Crossing the threshold
6. Tests, allies, enemies
7. Approach to the inmost cave
8. Ordeal
9. Reward
10. The road back
11. Resurrection
12. Return with the elixir

## When it works best
Stories of strong personal transformation, adventure, fantasy,
coming-of-age. It can be used non-literally (without "magic") for any
transformation story — the "ordinary world" can be emotional/social
rather than geographical.

## Caution
It can feel overused or forced when applied very literally to intimate
comedies or chamber dramas — in those cases it usually works better as
background inspiration, not a rigid template.
